# Privacy Policy for CleanJob (Job Filter)

*Last updated: September 6, 2026*

**CleanJob** ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
CleanJob does **NOT** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information, search history, web browsing activity, or device telemetry. 

## 2. Local Storage Usage
The Extension utilizes the standard chrome.storage.local API exclusively on your device to store:
- User-selected filter preferences (which categories are enabled/disabled).
- Custom user-defined filter keywords (words you choose to block).
- UI toggle states (e.g., Reveal Mode).

This data never leaves your computer and is never transmitted to our or any third-party servers.

## 3. Host Permissions
CleanJob requires access to job portal websites (olx.ua, olx.pl, pracuj.pl, work.ua, robota.ua, jooble.org, jooble.pl, gowork.pl, praca.pl) solely to inspect DOM elements and hide job postings that match your chosen filter criteria. The Extension does not modify, record, or track any other aspect of your browsing session.

## 4. Third-Party Services & External Links
The Extension does not use third-party tracking scripts, cookies, analytics services, or remote code. All application code and brand icons are packaged locally within the extension.

The Extension popup contains optional partner/referral links (such as TransferGo and Omio). When a user voluntarily clicks on these links, they are opened in a new browser tab directly on the respective third-party websites, which operate under their own privacy policies. CleanJob does not transmit any personal data, device information, or browsing history to these third-party platforms.
